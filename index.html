The story

From Jamshedpur to the systems banks run on.

I started with HTML footers and to-do apps. At Lovely Professional University I filled a GitHub with clones, DSA solutions and small React projects — the kind of repetition that makes fundamentals automatic.

Then the work got real. An upGrad internship turned MERN into muscle memory. At Tech Mahindra I shipped an internal project-management system and vendor admin panels that cut a department's maintenance cost by 30%.

Now I build lending infrastructure. At Lentra I work on the loan management and origination systems used by India's banks and NBFCs — disbursement, repayment, ledgers, reporting — where correctness isn't a feature, it's the whole job.
